# Bella Nissa Science

## Brand Direction

**Bella Nissa Science** is positioned as a premium, evidence-led skincare system pairing a high-performance serum with a purpose-built absorption and massage device. The identity moves away from conventional beauty cues and toward **quiet clinical credibility**: precise, calm, intelligent, and materially refined.

> The design language combines frosted-white skincare packaging, silver clinical hardware, and disciplined typography to signal laboratory-minded performance without feeling cold or generic.

## Brand Lockup and Typography

The wordmark is arranged on two lines. **Bella Nissa** appears first in a clean, modern humanist or neo-grotesk sans-serif, while **SCIENCE** is centered directly beneath it at a smaller size with generous letter spacing. This establishes the brand name as the lead identifier and makes the science platform unmistakable.

| Element | Recommended treatment | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Bella Nissa** | Refined medical sans-serif; regular or medium weight | Primary brand name |
| **SCIENCE** | Smaller uppercase sans-serif with open tracking | Clinical platform descriptor |
| **BNS monogram** | Interlocked geometric letters in a thin circular badge, with minimal molecular-node accents | Scientific signature and product stamp |
| Supporting text | Uppercase/technical sans-serif, restrained hierarchy | Product and technology information |

## Color and Material System

The updated concepts retain the original cool-white sensibility but strengthen it into a more credible science-forward palette. **Frosted white** carries the serum bottle and device body; **satin silver** is reserved for the dropper collar, foil label typography, monogram, and technical trim; **cool mist grey** supports secondary surfaces and digital applications. The result is more clinical and premium than a typical cosmetic palette while remaining soft and approachable.

| Color/material | Suggested value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frosted white | `#F4F6F6` | Primary packaging and device finish |
| Soft cool grey | `#DCE3E5` | Secondary backgrounds and contours |
| Satin silver | `#A7ADB0` | Foil lettering, monogram, collar, fine trim |
| Deep graphite | `#374047` | Digital body copy and high-contrast applications only |

## Product Application

The serum concept uses a semi-opaque frosted-white glass bottle with a **satin-silver dispenser collar** and retained white squeeze top. The label is intentionally sparse: the Bella Nissa / Science lockup, the BNS seal, the product category, and one ingredient-platform cue. Silver typography provides a precise, high-end clinical effect without excessive decoration.

The massage and absorption device remains a cool-white compact form. The power button stays central and the **BNS circular scientific badge** sits immediately below in subtle silver, followed by the small stacked wordmark. This placement turns the logo into a functional trust cue rather than an oversized cosmetic graphic.

## Production Notes

For final packaging, use true metallic silver foil or a high-opacity silver ink over frosted surfaces, not flat grey printing. The BNS seal should remain simple enough to engrave, emboss, laser-mark, or render at small scale. Before production, validate minimum line weights, foil trapping, and contrast on the actual frosted substrate.

The delivered images are **visual concepts**, intended to guide packaging, product-design, and brand-development decisions. Final logo artwork and packaging files should be rebuilt as vector assets before manufacture.
